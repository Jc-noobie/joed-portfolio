# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Joed for a junior or full-stack role or internship; freelance and business clients considering him for a paid project. Both audiences matter equally.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Joed Donaire that presents his projects, background, availability, and contact channels. Success means a visitor reaches out through the existing links (Gmail, GitHub, LinkedIn) rather than an in-page form.

## Positioning

The portfolio presents itself as an interactive desktop OS rather than a scrolling page: visitors open draggable windows for About, Works, FAQ, and Links. The desktop/window metaphor is the durable identity; the visual styling is open to change.

## Operating Context

Visitors use it in a browser on desktop or mobile. Joed is a 4th-year college student in the Philippines, open to internships, freelance work, and full-time roles, remote or onsite, typically replying within 24 hours on weekdays. His work spans church, company, and personal systems built with React, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Tailwind, and related tooling.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Window panels: About, Works, FAQ, Links, opened from a home card and tracked in a taskbar.
- Projects are ongoing systems (church, company, personal), not finished or shipped client work; copy must not imply otherwise.
- The Contact panel is empty and its story is undecided; the Express/nodemailer backend is a stub and unused.
- Experience data is placeholder content.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Joed Donaire.
- Tagline: "Web Developer | Team Leader | System Builder".
- Existing profile photo and social/contact links (GitHub, LinkedIn, Gmail) are the real contact path.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real project entries with GitHub links in `src/data/portfolio.js`.
- Profile photo at `src/Assets/profile.png`; pixel-art city backdrop and character assets under `src/Assets/`.
- FAQ facts about availability, role preferences, and reply cadence.
- Absences not to fabricate: no testimonials, no shipped-client proof, no real employment history, no pricing or benchmarks.

## Product Principles

1. The work and the person are the product; the interface should not outshine them.
2. Every claim must trace to a real project, link, or confirmed fact.
3. Contact stays frictionless through the existing links until a form is deliberately decided on.
4. The desktop metaphor is permanent; its visual expression may evolve.
5. Serve job seekers and clients without favoring one audience's path.
